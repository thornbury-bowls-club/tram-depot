Hi everyone,

We're launching The Tram Depot, a new online marketplace and noticeboard just for Thornbury Bowls Club members.

WHAT IT'S FOR

● Marketplace: buy, sell or give away bowls gear, food and produce, furniture, craft and services, or post a "Wanted" if you're looking for something.
● Bowlers wanted: short a player for social bowls or a tournament? Post it and find a fill-in.
● Social: share non-bowling events, like a trivia team or a footy outing.

You can ask a question on any listing, and the answers are visible to all members. There are no payments on the site: you sort out the details with each other, just as you would at the club.

HOW TO SIGN IN

There's no password to remember.

  1. Enter the email address this message was sent to.
  2. You'll get an email from The Tram Depot with a 6-digit code. If it hasn't arrived within a minute, check your junk folder.
  3. Type in the code and you're in. You'll stay signed in on that device, so you'll only need a new code if you sign out or use a different phone or computer.

If the site says your email isn't on the member list, reply to this email and let us know, and we'll sort it out.

SAVE IT AS AN APP

Put the Depot on your phone's home screen so it opens like an app:

● iPhone or iPad: open the link in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
● Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu at the top right, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
● Computer: just bookmark it.

A FEW HOUSE RULES

● It's for members only, so please keep it friendly and club-appropriate.
● When something sells, tap "Mark as sold". Listings also come down by themselves when they expire.
● If anything looks wrong, use "Report this listing" and a moderator will take a look.
